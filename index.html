Capabilities

What This Project Does

🔁
Automated ETL Pipeline
Extracts raw CSV sales data, cleans and transforms it using Python, and loads it into a structured SQL database.
🧹
Data Cleaning & Validation
Handles missing values, removes duplicates, fixes data types and standardises formats for consistent reporting.
🗄️
SQL Staging & Reporting Views
Organises data into staging tables and reporting views that Power BI connects to directly.
📊
Power BI Dashboard
Interactive dashboard with filters for branch, product category, date range and customer segments.
📈
Sales Trend Analysis
Daily, weekly and monthly sales charts to track revenue growth and identify seasonal patterns.
🏪
Branch Performance
Side-by-side comparison of all supermarket branches to flag high and low performers.
🛍️
Product Insights
Top and bottom performing products by category, margin and volume across time periods.
👥
Customer Analysis
Purchase frequency, average basket size and customer segmentation for targeted decisions.
